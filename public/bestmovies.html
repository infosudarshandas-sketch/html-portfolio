THE BEST MOVIES ACORDING TO SUDARSHAN

My Top Movies of All Time

SPIDER-MAN : BRAND NEW DAY

MY FAVORATE MOVIE RANK ONE. If you needed proof that Spider-Man: Brand New Day could be the biggest movie of the summer, it's already set the record for the biggest trailer launch ever. Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings and “Wonder Man” director Destin Daniel Cretton helms Spider-Man's return, picking up with Peter Parker now forgotten by his friends and fighting crime until a surprising physical evolution threatens his existence. Tom Holland, Zendaya, and Jacob Batalon return alongside MCU alumni Jon Bernthal and Mark Ruffalo, plus (hopefully) Sadie Sink. We're all really hoping those Jean Grey rumors are true!

THE ODESSEY

MY FAVORATE MOVIE RANK TWO. Summer movie season doesn't get more epic than a new Christopher Nolan film based on one of the most well-known stories in history, filmed entirely in 70mm IMAX. Matt Damon leads a stacked cast as the Greek king Odysseus as he journeys home to reunite with his wife, Penelope (Anne Hathaway). This will be the most fantastical story we've seen from Nolan, and with an estimated $250 million budget, his cinematic interpretations of things like the Trojan Horse, Greek gods, sirens, and mythical beasts should make for some eye-popping spectacle worthy of a trip to the cinema.

THE END OF OAK STREET

MY FAVORATE MOVIE RANK THREE. It's as if David Robert Mitchell created a sinkhole between "Stranger Things" and the shamefully good series "La Brea" for me to fill in with my hopes for his successful return to filmmaking. (The writer-director whiffed with Under the Silver Lake after his previous movie, It Follows, set an early standard for studios like Neon, A24, etc.)
